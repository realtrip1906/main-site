West Bengal

Darjeeling

3 Days · 2 Nights ₹5,500/person

Nestled in the foothills of the Eastern Himalayas, Darjeeling is one of India's most loved hill stations. Known as the Queen of Hills, it offers breathtaking views of Mt. Kanchenjunga, rolling tea gardens, colonial charm, and the spectacular sunrise from Tiger Hill. Perfect for family vacations, honeymoon trips, and relaxing hill getaways.

Day 1: NJP/Siliguri to Darjeeling

Pickup from NJP Railway Station / Siliguri and drive to Darjeeling via Kurseong Road.

  • Rohini View Point
  • Dow Hill (Kurseong)
  • Check-in at hotel in Darjeeling
  • Lunch & Dinner at hotel
  • Overnight stay at Darjeeling

Day 2: Darjeeling Local Sightseeing

Early morning sunrise at Tiger Hill followed by full day local sightseeing.

  • Tiger Hill Sunrise
  • Batasia Loop
  • Japanese Temple & Peace Pagoda
  • Ghum Monastery
  • Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park
  • Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (HMI)
  • Tenzing Rock
  • Ropeway
  • Tea Garden
  • Overnight stay at Darjeeling

Day 3: Darjeeling to NJP via Mirik

After breakfast, check-out and proceed towards NJP/Siliguri with en-route Mirik sightseeing.

  • Mirik Lake
  • Gopaldhara Tea Estate
  • Simana View Point (Indo-Nepal Border)
  • Nepal Border
  • Lepchajagat
  • Drop at NJP Railway Station / Siliguri

✅ Includes

  • Accommodation (Standard/Deluxe)
  • Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
  • Personal Cab (NJP to NJP)
  • All sightseeing as per itinerary

❌ Excludes

  • Outside food & Mineral water
  • Room heater & BBQ charges
  • Parking & Entry fees
  • Adventure activities
  • Ropeway & Toy Train ride

Darjeeling Offbeat

3 Days · 2 Nights ₹4,500/person

Darjeeling Offbeat destinations like Sittong and Lepchajagat offer a peaceful escape away from crowded tourist spots. Surrounded by pine forests, orange orchards, tea gardens, and panoramic Himalayan views, these hidden gems are perfect for travelers seeking silence, nature, and authentic village experiences.

Day 1: NJP / Siliguri to Sittong

Pickup from NJP Railway Station / Siliguri and drive towards Sittong (The Orange Village).

  • Monastery visit
  • Ahaldara View Point
  • Namthing Pokhri
  • Check-in at Sittong Homestay
  • Dinner & Overnight stay at Sittong

Day 2: Sittong to Lepchajagat

After breakfast, proceed towards Lepchajagat with scenic en-route stops.

  • Lovers Meet View Point
  • Gumbadara View Point
  • Peshok Tea Garden
  • Lamahatta Eco Park
  • Check-in at Lepchajagat Homestay
  • Dinner & Overnight stay at Lepchajagat

Day 3: Lepchajagat to Siliguri

After breakfast, proceed towards NJP / Siliguri with beautiful sightseeing stops.

  • Lepchajagat Pine Forest
  • Simana View Point (Indo-Nepal Border)
  • Pasupati Market
  • Nepal Border
  • Gopaldhara Tea Garden
  • Mirik Lake
  • Drop at NJP / Siliguri
