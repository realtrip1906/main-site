# Real Trip – Curated Travel Experiences

Welcome to **Real Trip**, your gateway to authentic travel experiences across India's most stunning destinations. We specialize in curated journeys, authentic homestays, and personalized travel planning across the Himalayas, Andaman Islands, and beyond.

## About Real Trip

Real Trip is a boutique travel company dedicated to creating unforgettable, authentic travel experiences. We work directly with local communities, expert guides, and trusted partners to deliver journeys that go beyond standard tourism.

### Our Specialties

- **Curated Expeditions**: 13+ destination circuits from Darjeeling and Sikkim to Kashmir, Spiti, Andaman, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, and more
- **Authentic Homestays**: Family-run accommodations with local hospitality and fresh mountain meals
- **Expert Planning**: Personalized itineraries tailored to your travel style
- **24/7 Ground Support**: On-trip assistance from local teams who understand mountain routes, weather, and permits

## Destinations

### Himalayan Regions
- **Darjeeling & Offbeat**: Tea gardens, misty mountains, offbeat villages
- **Silk Route Sikkim**: Historic trading routes, monasteries, mountain passes
- **North Sikkim**: Alpine landscapes, pristine peaks, sacred sites
- **West Sikkim**: Waterfalls, alpine meadows, mountain hospitality
- **Kalimpong**: Hill station charm, tea estates, panoramic views
- **Himachal Pradesh**: Alpine runs, adventure trails, cultural experiences
- **Spiti Valley**: Desert mountains, ancient monasteries, silent skies

### Coastal & Island Destinations
- **Andaman & Nicobar Islands**: Pristine beaches, water activities, island culture
- **Meghalaya**: Living root bridges, waterfalls, the wettest place on Earth

### Other Regions
- **Kashmir Valley**: Alpine meadows, houseboats, mountain majesty
- **Arunachal Pradesh**: Northeast adventures, tribal culture, pristine nature
- **Dooars**: Tea plantations, wildlife, river valleys
