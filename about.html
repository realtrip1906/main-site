Who We Are

We Are Travel
Artisans.

Real Trip was founded by a group of passionate travellers and local experts who believed that travel should be more than just ticking off destinations. Every journey we craft is deeply personal, deeply local, and deeply responsible.

We operate across the Himalayan belt — Darjeeling, Sikkim, Kalimpong, North Bengal, the Silk Route, Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Kashmir, Spiti — and the tropical paradise of the Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
