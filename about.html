How Real Trip
Became Real.

Chapter 01 · The Beginning

Started With Weekend Mountain Runs

What began as personal route experiments around Darjeeling and Sikkim became a repeat request from friends and families: "Can you plan ours too?" That demand shaped the first version of Real Trip.

Chapter 02 · The Ground Team

Built On Local Trust, Not Aggregators

Instead of generic vendor lists, we built direct relationships with drivers, homestays, and local operators. This gave us better reliability during weather changes and permit-heavy mountain routes.

Chapter 03 · The Travel Philosophy

Comfort + Authenticity In The Same Trip

We design itineraries that feel smooth for families yet meaningful for explorers. That means practical logistics, honest costing, and experiences that still feel local and personal.

Chapter 04 · Today

From Darjeeling Roots To Pan-India Circuits

Now we curate journeys across Sikkim, Northeast India, Himachal, Kashmir, and Andaman with one goal: make every guest feel guided, safe, and deeply connected to the destination.

Who We Are

We Are Memory
Creators.

Real Trip is a dedicated travel company committed to delivering memorable and hassle-free travel experiences across India's most breathtaking destinations. We specialize in curated tours for Darjeeling, Sikkim (North, East, West & Silk Route), Kalimpong, Dooars, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Himachal Pradesh, Kashmir, and Andaman.

With strong local networks and ground-level expertise, we ensure smooth travel arrangements with comfortable stays, reliable transportation, and guided sightseeing. Every itinerary is designed to balance comfort, exploration, and unforgettable moments.

What started as a passion for exploring hidden corners of Darjeeling and Sikkim grew into a mission: to help travelers experience mountains with trust, transparency, and complete support from pickup to drop.
