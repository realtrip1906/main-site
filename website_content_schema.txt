Overview : 
Nestled in the foothills of the Eastern Himalayas, Darjeeling is one of India’s most loved hill stations. Known as the Queen of Hills, it offers breathtaking views of Mt. Kanchenjunga, rolling tea gardens, colonial charm, and cool mountain weather throughout the year.
One of the main highlights of Darjeeling is the spectacular sunrise from Tiger Hill, where the snow-clad peaks glow golden in the early morning light. The town is also famous for its world-renowned tea estates and the iconic Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (Toy Train), a UNESCO World Heritage attraction.
With peaceful monasteries, scenic viewpoints, beautiful gardens, and vibrant local markets like Mall Road, Darjeeling is perfect for:

3. Silk Route : 
Overview :


The Silk Route in East Sikkim is a historic mountain trail that once connected India with Tibet for ancient trade. Today, it is one of the most breathtaking high-altitude destinations in Sikkim, famous for its dramatic zigzag roads at Zuluk, golden sunrises from Thambi View Point, and the serene beauty of Gnathang Valley and Kupup Lake.
With peaceful Himalayan villages, untouched landscapes, and panoramic views of snow-covered peaks, the Silk Route is perfect for travelers seeking adventure, raw nature, and a truly offbeat mountain experience.
